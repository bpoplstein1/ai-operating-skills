# Contributing

Start with a focused issue describing the workflow, expected result, and what happened. Use synthetic or sanitized inputs; do not publish customer data or private company notes.

For a new skill, explain the specific task it handles and include a small example with a reviewable expected outcome. Keep instructions self-contained, evidence-aware, and bounded to the user's request.

For fixes, describe the failure and the smallest change that resolves it. Review the [evaluation guide](docs/evaluation.md). Do not claim compatibility or performance that you have not tested.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution can be distributed under this repository's MIT license. Credit prior work and retain applicable notices when adapting it.
