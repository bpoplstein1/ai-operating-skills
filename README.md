# AI Operating Skills
### From The Output · By Brandon Poplstein

**Free AI skills for building products and running companies.**

Turn meeting notes and project updates into a useful operating routine: prepare for the day, capture what changed, and review the week.

Built for founders, product leaders, and teams who want a practical starting point for working with AI. Start with a few notes. Add integrations only when the routine is useful.

## Try your first skill

1. Open [Leadership Brief](skills/leadership-brief/SKILL.md) and copy its instructions into your AI assistant.
2. Add the fictional [company context](examples/northstar/context.md) and [source notes](examples/northstar/source-notes.md).
3. Ask: **“Prepare my leadership brief for September 14, 2026, using only these sources. Show what needs a decision and what is still unknown.”**
4. Compare the result with the [worked example](examples/northstar/worked-example.md).

You do not need to connect Gmail, Slack, or any other service to try this.

## Four skills. One routine.

| Skill | When to use it | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| [Chief of Staff](skills/chief-of-staff/SKILL.md) | Every day and every week, on a cadence | Three routines from one context file: morning brief, end-of-day debrief, weekly plan |
| [Leadership Brief](skills/leadership-brief/SKILL.md) | Before starting the day or a leadership meeting | Priorities, decisions, blockers, and evidence gaps |
| [Meeting Follow-Through](skills/meeting-follow-through/SKILL.md) | After a meeting | Confirmed decisions, explicit commitments, and proposed knowledge updates |
| [Weekly Review](skills/weekly-review/SKILL.md) | At the end of a week | Changes against priorities, unresolved issues, and next-week focus |

All four use the same [shared context template](docs/shared-context.md). The chief of staff is the three daily and weekly routines run as one cadence; its worked example is [here](examples/northstar/chief-of-staff-example.md). Keep your real company context in a separate private location, not in this public repository.

## How to use the library

The skills are plain Markdown instructions. Copy them into an assistant with your selected source material, or let a file-capable assistant read the relevant `SKILL.md` directly. See the [usage guide](docs/usage.md).

This first release is a manual-input starter. It does not install connectors, monitor your tools, schedule jobs, or change external systems. Native installation and automatic skill discovery depend on your assistant; cross-platform installation has not been verified for this release.

## What makes the output useful

- Separate a confirmed decision from a suggestion.
- Keep evidence attached to important claims.
- Surface conflicting information instead of quietly choosing a version.
- Leave missing owners, dates, and metrics visibly unknown.
- Propose updates for review rather than treating meeting discussion as permission to change systems.
